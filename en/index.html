Three tools, one computation

The solver says what you should have done. The tracker measures what you did, against exactly the same numbers. The trainer makes you replay your mistakes until they are gone. One single reference from end to end — that is what makes the cost of a leak believable.

Solve: the solver

Spin, fully covered

No Spin spot catches you out. All 14 stack depths from 15 to 2 BB, three-handed and heads-up, every option priced in big blinds, and big-jackpot mode computed in ICM and in euros of your buy-in.

6-max tournaments

Replay the hand as at the table: click each player's action before you, then read your strategy. From 100 to 10 BB with ante, opens, limps, calls, squeezes, 3bets through 5bets, bubble and final-table ICM, and your own stacks if you enter them.

Opponent profiles by seat

At your table, nobody plays equilibrium. Seat each opponent where he sits — eight types calibrated on real statistics, or a player from your own database in one click — and the ranges are recomputed for that table. A nit in the big blind does not change your game the way the same nit under the gun does.

My ranges

Got your own plan on some spots? Save it. The tracker then judges your decisions against your ranges rather than the solver's — but it still prices the loss with the solver's, so the figure stays honest.

Track: the tracker and the HUD

Live tracking

You watch your session build hand after hand. The app finds your hand-history folders by itself, shows the result in chips, big blinds or euros, and warns you when the session is going wrong.

You choose what you want to see on each opponent, and nothing else. Seventeen statistics, their order, one or two lines, size, colors, transparency — and on click, his full profile with his curve, his player type and your notes.

Opponents and reports

Improve: what makes you better

Your leaks, in numbers

The question nobody answers: where does the money go? Every preflop decision is compared with the computation, and your leaks are ranked by what they really cost you, in big blinds. You work on the first one on the list, not the one that annoys you.

Your seats and your hands

You may be losing from one seat only. Your result at each seat is compared with that of the other players sitting in the same seat in your games, with the margin of chance — so you know whether it is a real hole or just noise.

Variance, explained

Bad run or bad play? Your real winnings and your all-in EV on the same graph, with standard deviation, confidence interval and your worst downswing. Every figure has its explanation on click: none of them is there just to look serious.

How Solve It Poker computes

A solver that will not say how it computes is asking to be taken on trust. Here is the method, and what it cannot do.

  1. Measured equitiesEvery match-up between two hands is simulated tens of thousands of times.
  2. A computed equilibriumThe CFR algorithm looks for the strategy no player gains by deviating from.
  3. A checked resultIn 3-player Spin, at each of the 14 depths, no seat gains more than a thousandth of a BB by deviating from the computed strategy (up to fifteen thousandths heads-up above 10 BB). Measured inside the model.
  4. Calibrated profilesEach opponent profile is calibrated against typical statistic targets (VPIP, raise, limp, 3-bet, fold to 3-bet) at several depths; you can also set the solver on the real statistics of a player from your database.

What the software covers

  • Spin up to 15 BB, 3-handed and heads-up, winner-takes-all or big jackpot.
  • 6-max tournaments from 100 to 10 BB with ante, and bubble and final-table ICM.
  • Equal or different stacks, and opponents who stray from equilibrium.

Why preflop only

  • In Spin and in short-stacked tournaments, most of the money is decided before the flop: stacks are short, decisions are shoves or folds, and an opening mistake is paid for straight away. That is where a computation can be right, and that is where all the work went.
  • Postflop play is estimated by a model, not solved — we write it down rather than let you assume otherwise.
  • Raise sizes are simplified, and an opponent profile describes a type of player, not a person.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Solve It Poker while I play?

Not the solver: it is forbidden while playing, and Solve It Poker locks it automatically as soon as a table is open, until two minutes after your last hand. The tracker and the HUD depend on your room's rules: some allow software that records your statistics and those you have gathered on your opponents, others keep a list of approved software. As long as Solve It Poker is not on it, don't use its HUD at their tables — importing your hand histories after the session is still possible.

Which plan should I choose?

Solve to study ranges, Track to analyse your hands and use the HUD, or Improve to get everything, with direct access to the right range from every mistake. Every plan covers Spin and tournaments.

Can I change plan during my subscription?

Yes, at any time, from your customer area. The difference is prorated on your next bill.

Which computers does it run on?

On Mac with macOS 12 (Monterey) or later, Apple silicon or Intel, and on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PCs.

Which rooms does the tracker read?

Winamax and PokerStars (English hand histories for PokerStars), with live tracking and euro winnings read from tournament summaries. Other rooms may be added based on subscriber requests. Rooms don't offer a direct connection to third-party tools: Solve It Poker reads the files your poker client saves.

Do my euro winnings match my account?

Not necessarily. They are read from the tournament summaries on your computer: tournaments played on another machine, and those whose files your poker client deleted, are not included. Your account's exact balance is your room's cashier history.

How do opponent profiles work?

Each profile reproduces the statistics of a player type. On the solver's small table, you choose the profile of each seat: the solver computes the best response to that exact table, with seats left on "Equilibrium" playing the reference ranges. From a tracker opponent's sheet, "Seat him at the solver table" puts his real statistics in the seat you choose.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for computing: every grid and every analysis runs on your computer, without sending your hands. The connection is only used to check your subscription now and then.

Do the ranges change with the buy-in?

No: a decision depends on the stacks, the blinds and the payout structure. The buy-in and multiplier are used to show the value in euros in jackpot mode. Opponents' habits do vary by stakes: that's what profiles are for.

Which hands does the tracker analyse?

Spin (3-player and heads-up, up to 15 BB: Expresso on Winamax, Spin & Go on PokerStars) and 6-max tournament tables (100 to 2 BB). It judges your preflop decisions; after the flop, it tracks your winnings and EV but does not judge your decisions yet.

Does the tracker send my hands anywhere?

No. Hand histories are read and kept on your computer. You can delete them from the tracker at any time.

How does the free trial work?

Two ways to try it. Without paying anything: download the software, training is open for ten hands a day, no subscription, no card, no time limit. To try everything: 7 days on the plan of your choice, with your bank card. Nothing is charged during the trial, and you get a reminder e-mail before it ends. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the subscription starts automatically.

How do I cancel?

In three clicks: "Cancel your subscription" (at the top and bottom of every page), enter your name and e-mail, then confirm. You get an e-mail acknowledgement with the end date, and you keep access until the end of the paid period.

Can I play or bet from Solve It Poker?

No. Solve It Poker is analysis software: it computes decision charts and reads the hand-history files already on your computer. It does not run any game, take any wager or hold any funds, and it never connects to a poker site. To play, you use your usual poker client, separately.

Is Solve It Poker affiliated with Winamax or PokerStars?

No. Solve It Poker is independent software, neither affiliated with nor endorsed by Winamax or PokerStars.
