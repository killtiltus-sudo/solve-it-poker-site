Button at 20 BB, folded to you

Key takeaways

  • The button plays 40.3% of hands: 28.1% as a raise to 2 BB, 12.2% as a direct all-in.
  • The direct shove mostly goes to hands that dislike facing a reraise: small pairs (22 to 66), suited connectors (JTs to 87s), medium offsuit aces (ATo to A7o, A5o). Among suited aces, only A4s is shoved.
  • Very strong hands (88 to AA, AKs, AQs, KQs) raise to 2 BB to get paid, along with suited kings and a few offsuit hands (K8o, K7o, Q9o, A3o) that can be given up against a reraise. Offsuit AK and AQ raise to 2 BB too; KQo, AJo and KJo are split between the two, most often shoved.

Calculation assumptions

  • Chip EV, with no payout structure. ICM is set in the software, with “Solve my spot”.
  • Six players with equal stacks, table ante of about 1 BB in total.
  • Preflop calculation: play after the flop is estimated by a model, not solved.
