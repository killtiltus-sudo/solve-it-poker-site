Help and installation

Installing Solve It Poker

Which file should I download?

  • Mac (Apple silicon or Intel, macOS 12 or later): Solve It Poker for Mac (.dmg). One file for every Mac.
  • Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit): Solve It Poker for Windows (.exe)

On a Mac

  1. Double-click the downloaded .dmg file. A window opens with the Solve It Poker app and the Applications folder.
  2. Drag Solve It Poker onto the Applications folder.
  3. Close the window, then eject "Solve It Poker" from the Finder sidebar (click the ⏏ button next to it).
  4. Open Solve It Poker from the Applications folder. The first time, macOS asks whether you really want to open an app downloaded from the internet: click Open. After that, it opens normally.

On Windows

  1. Double-click the downloaded .exe file.
  2. Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (this is the Windows SmartScreen filter): click More info, then Run anyway. This message appears because the app is not yet signed for Windows: it does not say the app is dangerous, only that Microsoft does not know it.
  1. Follow the installation steps.
  2. Launch Solve It Poker from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

Finding your hands

The app finds your hand histories by itself and also analyses the ones already on your computer. If it can't find them: Tracker tab > Choose a folder.

  • Winamax: Options > Tracker shows the hand history folder.
  • PokerStars: Settings > Playing History > Hand History > tick "Save My Hand History" and "Save My Tournament Summaries", and choose English as the hand history language.
  • Tournament winnings in euros: Reports tab > Find my tournament results.

Getting the HUD to work

On a Mac: three permissions

The HUD needs to know where your tables are. macOS asks for your permission:

  1. Accessibility: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility, add "Solve It Poker" and turn it on.
  2. Automation: same section, "Automation", allow "System Events" for Solve It Poker.
  3. Screen Recording (if you play several Spin tables, or on Escape tables, where the HUD reads the screen names on screen): same section, "Screen & System Audio Recording" (just "Screen Recording" on macOS Sonoma), allow Solve It Poker. The app reads player names to recognise each table. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Then quit the app (⌘Q) and open it again.

If the HUD doesn't show even though Solve It Poker is turned on in Accessibility: remove it (–), add it again (+), turn it on, then restart the app. You only need to do this once: the app is signed with an Apple certificate, so the permission carries over from one update to the next.

On Windows

No permissions needed.

Tips for both

  • Winamax: turn off "playground" mode. It groups all tables into a single window, and the HUD can't be placed on it.
  • Several tables: if a HUD shows the players of another table, click "⇄ Wrong table?" at the top of the table and choose the right opponents.
  • Minimised tables, or tables on another desktop, have no HUD. When two tables overlap, only the HUD of the one in front stays visible.
  • PokerStars: don't use the HUD at their tables until Solve It Poker is on their list of approved software. You can still analyse your hands after the session.

Need help?

Email contact@solve-it-poker.com with:

  • a screenshot of the message or problem;
  • your system (Mac or Windows, and its version);
  • your poker room (Winamax or PokerStars).

If the HUD is in the wrong place: Tracker > My HUD > copy the list of detected windows, and send it with a screenshot of your table.
